# Clanker Killer
Author: Brent Peterson

### GameProgramProject

## Intro

This game takes place in the year 3067 where AI, robots and machines are everywhere in society. One day the AI and machines turn against humanity and rule the world. Your job is to play as the main character destroy the robots and save the world.

## Mechanics

The game is is semi-realistic FPS(First Person Shooter) story game. The player will use items that they find in the game to complete mission, defeat boss and other enemies that they find to progress in the story. 

### __Features__

* __FPS movements and gameplay__: Player will play in first person envoirment using various weapon and item to move forward in the story.
* __Health__: If player health falls to zero will die and respawn from last checkpoint.
* __Missions__: Players the missions or objective to move forward in the game.
* __Bosses__: Player must defeat bosses at each level to complete the level or chapter in the story.
* __Visual Aspects__: The game will contain auido and ui make the game feel more full. This give the player better experience player the game.
* __Menus__: There will be a start, option and pause menu. The start menu wil be the first thing the player sees. In start menu the player can play the game from a saved spot or start a new game. In the option menu players look at controls and adjust audio, graphics, and other settings to there liking.

## Dynamics

The player main goal to progresss through the story using items found in the game. To progress through the story players must defeat enemies and bosses to complete the levels or chapters.

## Aesthetics
The game will have semi-realistic look to it. Using real models to build the environment, weapon, items, and characters. The game will have a post apocalyptic cyberpunk look to it. The gunplay and movement will be based Destiny 2 and Halo.

## How to play
Use the WASD key to move around. 
Shifts + WASD to sprint in that direction
E key to interact with weapon crates and ammo boxes
Mouse 1 or Left click to shoot the weapon
Scroll Wheel or number 1, 2, 3 key to swap weapons.
R to reload
